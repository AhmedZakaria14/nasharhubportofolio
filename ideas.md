## Chosen Approach: Editorial Growth Atlas

### Design Movement
Modern editorial design blended with information design and restrained Saudi business sophistication. The interface should feel like a premium annual report that became interactive.

### Core Principles
1. Evidence before ornament: every visual treatment supports a case study, metric, or decision.
2. Asymmetric composition: offset columns, large editorial statements, and staggered cards avoid a generic centered landing page.
3. Quiet confidence: ink navy, ivory, signal blue, teal, and a controlled gold accent create trust without corporate blandness.
4. Motion as guided reading: animation reveals hierarchy and progression, never competes with content.

### Color Philosophy
Ink navy anchors the site with seriousness and contrast. Warm ivory keeps long Arabic reading comfortable. Signal blue marks links, progress, and forward motion. Teal is reserved for SEO and organic visibility. Gold is used sparingly for proof points and premium emphasis. The palette expresses a brand that is analytical, crafted, and commercially aware.

### Layout Paradigm
A vertical story with a persistent slim navigation rail on desktop, a compact top bar on mobile, and alternating split compositions. The hero uses a wide field with an offset content block. Case studies use editorial rows, image ledges, numbered markers, and horizontal evidence strips instead of uniform cards.

### Signature Elements
- Thin blue route lines that connect section markers like a growth map.
- Evidence cards with a small metric label, large Arabic interpretation, and a source tag.
- A recurring rounded-rectangle image frame with a clipped offset shadow, echoing the existing PDF mockups.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like turning pages in an intelligent report: hover reveals context, filters move the reader to a chapter, and buttons visibly respond. No interaction should hide core content or require guesswork.

### Animation
Use 180–320ms ease-out transitions for buttons and cards. On scroll, reveal section headings and evidence blocks with opacity plus a 14–20px translateY only. Stagger adjacent cards by 60ms. The hero includes a slow, subtle data-line drift and one-time entrance for the route line. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and disable decorative drift when requested.

### Typography System
Arabic display: Noto Kufi Arabic, 700–800, with tight line-height for major statements. Arabic body: Noto Sans Arabic, 400–600, generous line-height for explanation. Latin labels: Space Grotesk, 600–700, uppercase with tracking for section metadata. Never use Inter.

### Brand Essence
NasharHub turns digital presence into measurable growth for ambitious Saudi and regional businesses by combining crafted websites, search visibility, and performance marketing in one accountable story.
Personality: precise, composed, catalytic.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are decisive and insight-led. CTAs are specific and directional. Microcopy explains what the reader is seeing instead of using filler.

Example lines:
- «لا نعرض واجهة فقط؛ نُظهر القرار الذي جعلها تعمل.»
- «من حضور رقمي متفرق إلى مسار نمو يمكن قراءته.»

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the provided NasharHub wordmark for the word logo, paired with a standalone geometric upward-signal mark for the symbol. The symbol must appear at visible size in the header and as favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
Signal Blue #1677D2 — the ownable color of forward motion, clarity, and visible progress.

## Style Decisions

- اعتمدنا سكة أطلس رفيعة على سطح المكتب تربط فصول الرؤية والمواقع وSEO والإعلانات برقم ومؤشر واضحين، مع بقاء شريط الهاتف بسيطاً.
- أصبح رمز NasharHub الهندسي علامة فصل متكررة في كل محطة كبرى، بدل ظهوره حصراً في الرأس والنهاية.
- تُعرض الملاحظات المنهجية كـ«ملاحظات ميدانية» مرقمة ذات فواصل تحريرية، وليس كبطاقات منتجات متساوية.
- تختلف مادة كل فصل بوضوح: أرشيف حالات داكن، مكتب تحليل شبكي هادئ لـSEO، وغرفة قيادة مؤشرات ذات شبكة Signal Blue للإعلانات.
